#### 2. Academic Delivery Framework

This Special DR is conducted in accordance with the prescribed Academic Delivery mode, following a three-part structure:

- **One-third Classroom Teaching (15 hours):** Sessions will be conducted in **offline/online mode** as per the schedule. **Mandatory attendance is required.**
- **One-third Asynchronous Content Sharing (15 hours):** Self-study content, including the recommended MOOC, will be provided for independent learning.
- **One-third Project-Based Learning (15 hours):** A mandatory project component must be completed and submitted.

You are required to actively participate in all three components to successfully complete the course.

#### 4. Weekly Progress Reporting (Mandatory)

You are required to report your weekly learning and practical progress regularly.

- **Schedule:** The schedule, time, and venue for the weekly progress reporting will be communicated to you separately.

#### 6. Project Work (Major Component)

As part of the Project-Based Learning (PBL) framework, you are required to complete projects that demonstrate your understanding of the syllabus.

- **Total Projects:** You will be assigned **Four (4) Projects** covering different units of the syllabus.
- **Submission Requirement:** You must submit **any two (2)** of these projects for evaluation.
- **Coverage:** The projects will be designed to cover key topics from all units (jQuery, AngularJS, NodeJS, MongoDB, D3.js).
- **Details:** The specific problem statements, guidelines, and submission deadlines for the four projects will be shared separately during the first contact session.
