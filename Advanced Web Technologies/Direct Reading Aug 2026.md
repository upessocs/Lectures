#### 2. Course Duration and Academic Schedule

The Direct Reading process is governed by specific timelines depending on your graduating status. Strict adherence to these timelines is mandatory.

- **a. For Graduating Students (2026 Batch):**
    - **Duration:** August 2026 to October 2026.
    - **Mandatory Contact:** A minimum of **TWO contact hours per week** with the assigned faculty member is mandatory. Sessions can be conducted in offline or online mode.
    - **Withdrawal:** Withdrawal from this registered Directed Reading course will **not** be permitted after successful registration.
    - **End Term Examination:** The End Term Examination will be conducted in **Offline Mode** in October 2026.

- **b. For Non-Graduating Students:**
    - **Duration:** August 2026 to December 2026.
    - **Mandatory Contact:** A minimum of **ONE contact hour per week** with the assigned faculty member is mandatory.
    - **Withdrawal:** Withdrawal from this registered Directed Reading course will **not** be permitted after successful registration.
    - **End Term Examination:** The End Term Examination will be conducted in **Offline Mode** as per the directives from the COE's Office.

The coverage for quizzes and examinations is predefined. You are advised to prepare all units thoroughly and in a timely manner.

- **Quiz 1:** Unit 1 and Unit 2

- **Quiz 2:** Unit 4

#### 5. Assignments and Weekly Progress Reporting

- **Assignments:** All assignments must be prepared and submitted according to the instructions provided in the course resources.
- **Weekly Reporting:** You are required to report your weekly learning and practical progress regularly. The schedule, time, and venue/mode for these progress review sessions will be communicated to you during the following week. Maintain regular progress throughout the semester.

#### 6. Mandatory GitHub Repository

- **Repository Creation:** Each student must create a dedicated GitHub repository for this course. This repository will serve as the central location for all your work.
- **Maintenance:** You are required to regularly upload and maintain your assignments, practice programs, lab work, and project progress in this repository.
- **Evaluation:** Your GitHub repository and the consistency of your progress updates will be a significant component of your evaluation process.

#### 7. Git and GitHub Tutorial

Students who are new to Git and GitHub must first complete the following mandatory tutorial before starting their work:
[https://upessocs.github.io/#dir=/Lectures/Advanced%20Web%20Technologies/&file=001%20Git%20and%20GitHub%20Installation%20and%20Basics.md](https://upessocs.github.io/#dir=/Lectures/Advanced%20Web%20Technologies/&file=001%20Git%20and%20GitHub%20Installation%20and%20Basics.md)
You are strongly recommended to become proficient in basic Git operations, including repository creation, cloning, committing changes, pushing changes, and maintaining project history.

#### 8. Important Instructions and Compliance

- **Self-Discipline:** Do not wait until the examination period to complete the course material. Regularity, practical implementation, and consistent documentation of your work are critical for your success.
- **Compliance:** All academic records, including attendance, assessments, evaluations, and related documentation, must be maintained in compliance with institutional and audit requirements. Failure to adhere to these instructions will result in academic penalties
